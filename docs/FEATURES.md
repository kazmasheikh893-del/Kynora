# 3. AI Email Analysis

This is the core functionality of Kynora.

## MVP

### Email Summary

Generate concise summaries for long emails.

---

### Smart Reply Suggestions

Generate context-aware reply suggestions.

---

### Priority Detection

Automatically classify emails as:

- High Priority
- Medium Priority
- Low Priority

---

### Email Categorization

Automatically organize emails into categories such as:

- Work
- Personal
- Finance
- Education
- Promotions
- Social
- Updates

---

### Action Item Extraction

Detect actionable tasks from emails.

Example:

- Submit report
- Schedule meeting
- Complete payment
- Reply before Friday

---

### Deadline Detection

Identify important dates and deadlines mentioned within emails.

---

### Keyword Extraction

Highlight important keywords for faster understanding.

---

# Long-Term Product Vision

Kynora is designed to evolve beyond an email analyzer into a complete AI-powered productivity platform.

Future versions will combine intelligent email management, task automation, workflow optimization, scheduling, collaboration, analytics, and AI-powered decision support within a unified workspace, making Kynora a comprehensive personal and professional productivity assistant.
